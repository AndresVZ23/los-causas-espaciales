# Pitch

Climate and territorial conditions are changing, while investment decisions still depend on fragmented information. Los Causas Espaciales transforms NASA Earth observations into understandable evidence for real assets.

The user selects a real location. NASA POWER supplies the historical series. Our engine answers what is changing, where, by how much, and whether the change is statistically significant. The platform then connects that evidence to energy, infrastructure, territorial, and financial questions while keeping observations, model outputs, and user assumptions separate.

The difference is not another static risk map. It is traceability from every interpretation back to its observations, plus an explicit statement when evidence is insufficient. The MVP already retrieves real data, calculates Mann–Kendall and Sen slope, exposes source and units, validates project geometries, compares locations, and calculates NPV only from explicit inputs.

The next steps are sampled corridor and polygon analysis, GPM IMERG, AppEEARS, PostGIS, and verified official Peruvian layers. The vision is a regional territorial-intelligence platform for more resilient decisions without replacing engineering, environmental, archaeological, financial, or legal due diligence.
